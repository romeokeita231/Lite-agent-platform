Third-party components inside the bundled PostgreSQL distribution
-----------------------------------------------------------------
postgres.zip is the unmodified EDB Windows x64 binary distribution of PostgreSQL 16.4.
Besides PostgreSQL itself it ships these components, each under its own licence:

  icu*67.dll            ICU            Unicode licence
  libcrypto-3-x64.dll   OpenSSL        Apache-2.0
  libssl-3-x64.dll      OpenSSL        Apache-2.0
  libiconv-2.dll        GNU libiconv   LGPL-2.1
  libintl-9.dll         GNU gettext    LGPL-2.1
  libxml2.dll           libxml2        MIT
  libxslt.dll           libxslt        MIT
  liblz4.dll            LZ4            BSD-2-Clause
  libzstd.dll           Zstandard      BSD-3-Clause / GPL-2.0
  zlib1.dll             zlib           zlib licence
  wx*_vc_x64_custom.dll wxWidgets      LGPL-2.1 (pgAdmin 4 only)
  libwinpthread-1.dll   mingw-w64      permissive

carries is PostgreSQL's own, at doc/postgresql/html/legalnotice.html after extraction.
